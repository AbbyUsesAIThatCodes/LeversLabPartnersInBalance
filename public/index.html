Masses and Positions

Fulcrum

Negative is toward the beam’s marked left end. The fulcrum stays between the two objects.

Arm lengths are measured from the fulcrum. Steps: 25 mm and 25 g. Each arm stays at least 75 mm long. Use Swap Positions to exchange sides.

More Distance. Less Effort.

  1. Use Controls to change either mass or its distance from the fulcrum. Drag the gold Load, teal Effort, purple Fulcrum, or their labels along the beam.
  2. Hold Level and Hide Math let you predict. Release to see which side goes down, then reveal the calculation.
  3. Swap Positions exchanges exact beam coordinates. Each object keeps its own mass, color, shape, and role. The fulcrum stays still. Swap twice to return.

Keyboard: Tab to a role label, then use left/right arrows to move across the screen. Up/down changes Load or Effort mass. Sliders, number boxes, and ½/2× buttons are also available. Drag the background to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Side View and Fit View restore useful views.

Forces and motion: the gold Load presses on its supported tray; the teal hanging Effort pulls through its cord. Both applied forces point down, even when an object moves up. These descriptions fit this apparatus, not every possible lever.

Ideal model: only the two labeled masses count. The beam, tray, cord, and attachments are massless; the pivot is ideal. The load is constrained in a level, self-leveling holder so it cannot tip or slide. Both vertical force lines pass through their labeled points on the beam axis, with no extra torque from decorative offsets.

Distance and motion: arms run along the beam from the fulcrum to each force point. When tilted, both horizontal moment arms shrink by cos(angle), so the balance ratio stays the same. The math panel shows turning effects at level. Motion uses an illustrative point-mass inertia and damping, limited to ±12°; it is not a calibrated acceleration simulation.
