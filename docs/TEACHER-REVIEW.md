# Teacher Review Guide

Lever Lab is a separate development game for the complete owner-authored R06
Part 1 Q1–Q8 and Part 2 Q9–Q14. Optional A–E is not required. The simulation
retains the numerical physics and controls with a new hand/load representation,
frozen classroom environment, and matching diagram fallback.

## Playtest Sequence

1. Read the short partnership introduction and force calibration. Explain why
   a 100 g-equivalent push is not a 100 g hand. Begin the guided assignment.
2. Check the embedded instruction and worked example on each step. Make a factual
   error and confirm Continue waits. Open Vocabulary and return to the same state.
   Main and notebook controls remain usable. Drag down to increase load mass or
   effort push; sideways changes position. Check both keyboard alternatives.
3. Identify Effort, Load, and Fulcrum using the apparatus or keyboard buttons.
   A wrong choice resets the three-choice streak. All candidates share a blue cue.
   Use Hide Notebook / Show Notebook and the lever-icon Question Index.
   Classwork is anonymous and shared; the teacher guides partner routines externally.
4. Complete Q7 with a failed first prediction and a successful retry. Select
   your actual saved row in Q11; verify both the first prediction and final effort push.
5. Check Q12: IMA 2 requires half the load weight as effort at ideal balance;
   IMA 0.5 requires twice. First class does not guarantee IMA greater than one.
6. Test Q13's balance and lift distinction, then create a non-default Q14 design.
   Label your own sketch with its load mass, effort push, and arms, test, and explain your evidence.
7. Reload, download a backup, restore it, and inspect the Student Work report.
   Confirm drawings, originals, revisions, trial setups, retained changes and help use.

## What Feedback Means

**Checked**: constrained factual/numerical checks passed.
**Recorded · Teacher Review**: required evidence exists; explanation/drawing quality
needs human judgment. **Try Again**: a factual check or target trial needs revision;
the unsuccessful attempt remains saved. A prediction is never wrong for disagreeing
with the later result. Coverage completion is not an individual mastery score.

## Model And Transfer

At held level, opposing mass × arm equality predicts balance under shared gravity.
The g·mm products are not SI torque. The restoring pointer acts when tilted; a
passing level position is not settled balance. Centered arms stop at 250 mm,
off-center derived arms may reach 425 mm, and the model uses 25 g, 25 g-equivalent, and 25 mm steps.
The game prepares learners to choose and defend lever positions. Physical assembly,
friction, measurement error, and real testing remain separate experiences.

## Saving And Submission

Work stays in this browser. Download backups before
switching devices, browsers, or local preview ports. Invalid restore files are
rejected before replacing the open notebook. An unreadable autosave is kept for
recovery instead of silently overwritten. Pre-update and replaced-session raw copies
remain available through Recover Saved Work. Changed question objectives preserve
old evidence and mark current completion Needs Another Look.

After all question evidence is recorded, Download Completed Work produces one printable HTML file with embedded drawings and
machine-readable notebook data. Attach it to the Google Classroom assignment and
select Turn In. Lever Lab neither uploads nor verifies submission. There are no
accounts, telemetry, runtime CDNs, or student server records. The teacher writes
their own communications.

## Release Boundary

Automated review does not establish instructional readiness, official alignment,
HQSD approval, or classroom administration. Thorough teacher playtesting remains
required. No merge, deployment, email, or auto-merge is authorized by this work.
